tips_and_updates

Chef's Tip: Make sure the salmon is at room temperature before searing to ensure even cooking and a perfect crust.

Preparation Steps

1

Sear the Salmon

Season salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place salmon skin-side down and sear for 4-5 minutes until crispy. Flip and cook for another 2 minutes.

2

Aromatic Base

Remove salmon from the pan. Add butter and let it melt until foaming. Stir in minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant but not browned.

3

Create the Sauce

Lower the heat and pour in the heavy cream. Whisk gently and simmer for 2-3 minutes until the sauce slightly thickens. Stir in fresh lemon juice and chopped parsley.

4

Finish & Plate

Place the salmon back into the pan. Spoon the garlic butter sauce over the fillets. Garnish with more fresh dill and serve immediately with lemon wedges.
